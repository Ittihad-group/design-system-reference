The 17 HR templates in their English (left-to-right) edition, with the same specification as the Arabic originals in hr-documents.html (header, number, status, footer, stamp and signature, Latin numerals). All data is fictional and marked as a sample, and legal wording remains pending legal review. A plain PDF is not an approved electronic document. Employment contracts must still be executed in Arabic; English editions are courtesy copies.

Dates are YYYY-MM-DD, numerals are Latin, status = document lifecycle only, and confidentiality is a separate badge. The attendance sheet uses a landscape page.
