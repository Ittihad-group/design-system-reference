What PDF files look like in the Egyptian-German system — in English

The system issues every document in Arabic and in English. This page shows the 11 A4 templates in their English (left-to-right) edition: same header, footer, colours and layout as the Arabic originals in documents.html. Each template can be printed or saved as PDF from here and has a ready sample PDF.

Unified document specification

All documents share the same header, footer and colours, so any file leaving the system is clearly issued by Egyptian-German. The English edition uses the identical grid, mirrored for left-to-right reading.

Header · 28mm
Content
Footer · 14mm
A4 · 210×297mm · margins 14mm

Header

Colour horizontal logo, the document title in a navy pill, then document number, date and status. A short red line under the navy rule acts as the brand signature. The Arabic title appears as a small bilingual subtitle.

Footer

Address, hotline 15536, email and website, ISO and "Made in Egypt" badges, and the page number "1 / 3".

Fonts & sizes

Title 14pt · body 9.5pt · tables 9pt · minimum 8pt. Latin tabular numerals keep digits aligned.

Print-friendly

Table headers use a light navy tint; solid navy only for the title pill and the grand-total row. Reads clearly even in black and white.

Data from Settings

Tax ID · Commercial Register · stamp and signature · company details — entered once and shown on every document.

Document statuses

DraftConfirmedPartialPaidCancelled

Dealer Proforma Invoice

  • “A sales representative will call you” box and dealer number
  • Stages: submit → call → confirm → tax invoice
