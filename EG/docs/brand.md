# Brand

> Source: [egerman-upvc.com](https://egerman-upvc.com/) + `assets/images/logo/`

## Logo

### Mark (seal)

Circular badge: open door/window motif + **E** (red) + **G** (navy). Arabic arcs: المصرية الألمانية / لصناعة منتجات · WPC & PVC. Star separators on the sides.

File: `assets/images/logo/mark.png`

### Wordmark lockup

Mark + split wordmark: **المصرية** (red) · **الألمانية** (navy) · subtitle WPC & PVC / لصناعة منتجات.

Files: `wordmark-color.png` (default) · `wordmark-white.png` (on dark)

### Clear space / minimum

- Clear space ≈ height of the EG letters in the seal
- Minimum digital mark ≈ 32 CSS px
- Favicon: `favicon.png` / `mark-350.png`

### Don'ts

- Do not recolor E/G independently
- Do not stretch or rotate the seal
- Do not place color wordmark on busy photography without a solid plate
- Do not replace with a text-only “EG” in a different typeface for official chrome

## Color story

Red = Egyptian half, urgency, primary CTA (matches live site buttons `#ED1C24`).  
Navy = German half, structure, headings, secondary actions (`#0E2F64`).  
Together they read as the bilingual manufacturer identity.

## Voice

- Arabic: clear Modern Standard with light Egyptian clarity — industrial, not slangy
- English: professional B2B manufacturing tone
- Avoid hype (“الأفضل 100%”); prefer measurable product claims (moisture resistance, dimensions, ex-factory pricing)

## Imagery

- Real plant / expo / product photography (doors, cladding stacks, foam boards)
- Prefer daylight industrial honesty over stock “perfect home”
- Logo overlays: use reverse wordmark on dark plates

## Application

| Context | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| ERP shell | Navy sidebar/top · red primary buttons · mark 28–32px |
| Marketing hero | Wordmark or mark · red CTAs · product photography |
| Mobile splash | Centered mark on white or navy |
| Print / PDF | Color wordmark; seal for stamps |
