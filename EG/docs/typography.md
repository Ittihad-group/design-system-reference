## Arabic rules

- Line-height ≈ 1.7
- Avoid positive letter-spacing on Arabic
- Numerals: Latin tabular in both languages for prices/SKU

## English rules

- Line-height ≈ 1.55
- Headings slight tracking tighten OK (−0.01em)

## ERP density

Body can drop to 14px in dense tables; keep headers ≥ 13px; never below 12.5px.
