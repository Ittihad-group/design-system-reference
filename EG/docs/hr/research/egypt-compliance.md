## 0. Ten facts management must know

1. **New Labour Law No. 14/2025** was published in the Official Gazette (issue 18 tabi') on **3 May 2025**, in force **1 Sep 2025**, labour courts from **1 Oct 2025**. It repealed Law 12/2003 and Law 125/2010. [V-Text: issuing Arts. 12-13]
2. **Executive regime = a package of ministerial decisions, not one "executive regulation".** Issued in waves 2025-2026 (e.g. 294/2025 holidays, 162/2026-type work-regulation rules, 248-252/2026 OSH). Completeness is **not confirmed**. [R-Secondary]
3. **Private-sector minimum wage = EGP 7,000/month since 1 Mar 2025** (NCW). No newer decision as of 29 Oct 2026; next review "early 2027". [R-Secondary]
4. **Statutory annual raise (العلاوة الدورية) >= 3% of insurable wage** (Law 14 Art. 12) — NCW also set a floor of EGP 250/month (Mar 2025). Exemption only via the National Council for Wages. [V-Text + R-Secondary]
5. **Social insurance 2026:** employee 11% + employer 18.75%; insurable-wage band **EGP 2,700 – 16,700/month from 1 Jan 2026** (2025: 2,300 – 14,500). [V-Official NOSI 30 Nov 2025 + V-Text Law 148]
6. **Salary tax** (Law 91/2005 as amended): 0% to EGP 40,000 net, then 10/15/20/22.5/25/27.5%, plus EGP 20,000 personal exemption; **monthly withholding is now computed through the ETA unified payroll system (mandatory since 15 Dec 2024)**, paid by the 15th of the next month. [R-Secondary + V-Official ETA FAQ Mar 2026]
7. **Disability quota: 5% for employers with 20+ workers** (Law 10/2018 Art. 22) — NOT 50+ (50 was the old 2003 rule). For ~100 workers = 5 positions. [R-Secondary; conflicts with the premise in the brief]
8. **Hours/overtime:** 8 h/day, 48 h/week; overtime min **+35% day / +70% night**; work on weekly rest day = extra full day's pay + a compensatory day within the following week; work on official holiday = double pay or day in lieu on written request. [V-Text Arts. 117-129]
9. **Dismissal can only be ordered by the labour court** (Art. 148), except resignation/abandonment rules; disciplinary deductions capped at 5 days' basic wage per violation and per month. Written contract (Arabic, 4 copies) and pre-signed resignations are invalid. [V-Text]
10. **Annual leave 15 / 21 / 30 / 45 days; maternity 4 months x up to 3 times; new paternity day, 1-month Hajj leave, up to 2-year unpaid childcare leave** (50+ employees). [V-Text Arts. 54, 57, 124-130]

### 1.10 Disability quota — conflict resolved

* Brief premise: "5% for 50+ employees". **Not correct under current law.** Law 10/2018 **Art. 22** applies to government and non-government employers with **20 or more workers**, **5%** of total workforce (Dostor explainer, Clyde & Co note on Law 14 also says 20+). Law 12/2003 used 50+ — that is probably the origin of the "50".
* Penalty (Law 10/2018 Arts. 53-54, secondary source): jail min 6 months and/or fine EGP 10,000-30,000 on the responsible manager (amendment by Law 156/2021 not checked) [U].
* Law 14/2025 itself defers to Law 10/2018 (Art. 35, 37 require the register and reporting).
* **Setting:** `quota_pct = 5`, `quota_min_headcount = 20`, source = Law 10/2018 Art. 22.

### 2.3 What counts as insurable wage

Under Law 148 there is **no basic/variable split for contributions** (that split was Law 79/1975). The **single "insurable wage" (أجر الاشتراك)** [V-Text Art. 1(8)] consists of: functional wage, basic wage, supplementary wage, incentives, commissions, the "tip pool" if conditions met, **allowances** (except those listed below), **overtime pay**, compensation for extraordinary effort, cost-of-living aid, social raises, collective grants/bonuses, special raises not merged... all capped at the maximum.
**Excluded allowances:** transport, travel, session attendance, cost-reimbursing allowances (except representation); housing/clothing/car allowances given in lieu of in-kind benefits; secondment allowances; overseas cost-of-living allowances.
Labour Law 14 uses "insurable wage" (الأجر التأميني) as the base for the 3% annual raise and the training fund.

## 3. Payroll income tax (ضريبة كسب العمل — Law 91/2005 as amended)

### 3.1 Rates in force (2026)

Annual brackets applied to **annual net taxable salary** (after the personal exemption and permitted deductions):

| Annual net bracket (EGP) | Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 40,000 | **0%** |
| 40,001 – 55,000 | 10% |
| 55,001 – 70,000 | 15% |
| 70,001 – 200,000 | 20% |
| 200,001 – 400,000 | 22.5% |
| 400,001 – 1,200,000 | 25% |
| Over 1,200,000 | 27.5% |

* **Personal exemption: EGP 20,000/year** (residents and non-residents). Sources: PwC Tax Summaries (last reviewed 17 Aug 2026), Andersen (26 Jan 2026), Deloitte Bahrain alert on Law 7/2024 [R-Secondary].
* **Amendment chain** (secondary sources; official gazette texts not read):
  * **Law 175/2023** — effective **1 Nov 2023**: zero band raised from 21,000 to 30,000, 2.5% band removed; exemption stayed 15,000 (PaySpace) .
  * **Law 7/2024** (issued 21 Feb 2024) — effective **1 Mar 2024** (PaySpace): exemption **15,000 -> 20,000**, zero band to **40,000**.
  * ETA FAQ (Mar 2026) says tax is computed "per Art. 8 of Law 91/2005 and **Art. 72 bis added by Law 30/2023**, from **January 2024**, taking Art. 10 into account." So Law **30/2023** is also relevant to the 2024 bracket table. **Exact sequence between 30/2023, 175/2023 and 7/2024 is [U]**; the figures above are consistent across PwC, Andersen, PaySpace and Deloitte.
  * **No 2025-2026 change in rates found** (PwC reviewed Aug 2026 still shows the same table).
* Brief mentions "Law 175/2023? Law 30/2023?": **Both exist and both amended the bracket/exemption mechanics; Law 7/2024 is the one that set today's EGP 20,000 / 40,000 figures.**
* A "higher-income loses lower brackets" rule exists for annual incomes above EGP 600,000 (PaySpace, for the 2023 version) — irrelevant to blue-collar staff but should be modelled for managers [U].

### 3.2 Deductions

* Employee's share of **social insurance contributions** is deductible (Art. 10 of Law 91/2005 — via ETA FAQ reference; ETA also lists health-insurance premiums and life-insurance premiums (15% of net income, max EGP 10,000) as deductible items) [R/V-Official partial].
* Exempt: pensions, end-of-service gratuities and specified allowances (Andersen), collective in-kind benefits per Art. 13(5) are not entered in the monthly form (ETA FAQ).
* Transport allowance given as taxable allowance is entered in the form; real reimbursements are not (ETA FAQ).

### 3.3 Monthly withholding method (official ETA payroll system)

Source: **ETA "منظومة توحيد أسس ومعايير احتساب ضريبة الأجور والمرتبات" FAQ v4, March 2026** [V-Official].

* Mandatory for **all employers in Egypt since 15 Dec 2024** (Minister of Finance decision **386/2023**).
* The employer submits the **monthly calculation form (نموذج الاحتساب الشهري)** — one per payroll run, or one per month combining runs (Excel upload, ERP integration/API with SDK, or manual entry). The system computes the tax and issues a claim in ETA's Core system.
* **Method:** cumulative net income year-to-date = this period + previous periods, **annualised** (cumulative x 360 / actual working days), tax computed on the annual base using the brackets, then the monthly tax = cumulative tax less tax already withheld. Standard work period = 30 days per month.
* Payment: within **15 days after the month of deduction**; payment after the 15th triggers recalculation and a delay charge.
* The monthly form integrates automatically with the **monthly payment form** and **Form 2 مرتبات**; employers may no longer enter those manually.
* Negative numbers are not accepted; corrections go in the "pre-settlement processing" form or next month.
* **Annual settlement:** auto-populated from monthly forms; submitted via **Form 9 تسويات** on the Core system; an older (2021) source says "6 تسويات (private) / 7 تسويات (government) by end of January" — **trust ETA 2026 FAQ (Form 9)**. Deadline: end of January (2021 press) [U for 2026 date].
* Eight "tax treatments" exist in the system for different employment cases (e.g. treatment 2 = non-original employer, 3 = seconded staff / >50% income from non-original employer).
* Employee portal (pay-slip) available; employee registration is optional for the employer.

### 4.2 Statutory onboarding steps

1. Check/obtain the worker's **registration certificate** (Art. 33) — or register within 30 days.
2. Sign the **written Arabic contract (4 copies)**; deposit copies with NOSI office and the competent authority.
3. **NOSI registration** (Form 1 + Form 111; establishment file Form 2 already open); keep insurance proof in the worker file.
4. Create the **personnel file** (paper/electronic) with all Art. 92 content.
5. Enter the employee in the **ETA payroll tax system** (national ID, tax treatment code, insurance data); first monthly form.
6. Give the worker access to the **work regulation (لائحة تنظيم العمل)** (10+ workers) and safety instructions; PPE free of charge, OSH training before starting (Arts. 267-269).
7. Update the **workforce statement** (every January, Art. 36) and, if applicable, the disability register (Arts. 35, 37).
8. Probation <= 3 months, one time only.

## 6. Calendar — public holidays 2026 and attendance conventions

### 6.1 Rules

* Paid official holidays for private-sector workers are set by **Minister of Labour Decision 294/2025** (Art. 129): **12 occasions** — Hijri New Year; Mawlid; Eid al-Fitr (**2 days** in the list); Eid al-Adha (**3 days**: Arafat + first 2 days of Eid); Coptic Christmas (7 Jan); 25 January/Police Day; Sham el-Nessim; Sinai Liberation Day (25 Apr); Labour Day (1 May); 30 June; 23 July; Armed Forces Day (6 Oct). [R-Secondary: Youm7 2 Jan 2026; Dostor 16 Aug 2026]
* Minister may **replace a holiday by another day** (start/end of week) to unify with the government — in 2026 many were moved to **Thursday**. Holidays **do not count** from annual leave. Non-Muslim workers get their own religious holidays by Cabinet decision.
* Work on a holiday: **pay for the day + "مثلي" (twice) the wage, or a day in lieu on written request** (Art. 129).
* Each occasion is announced by a **Labour Ministry circular (كتاب دوري)**; private-sector dates may differ from government.

### 6.3 Attendance conventions (factory)

* Legal minimum: **24 h paid weekly rest after <= 6 days**; **Friday** is the customary day for private-sector, any other day allowed if posted (Art. 122). Law 133/1961 continues to apply to industrial establishments.
* Shifts: law fixes limits only (8 h effective, <= 10 h span, >= 1 h breaks, <= 12 h presence). Shift patterns (2-3 shifts, night differential) are by work regulation; **night hours definition is by decision [U]**.
* Ramadan: no private-sector statutory reduction; many factories cut ~1-2 h by policy (configurable).
* Absence rules: abandonment after 10 consecutive/20 intermittent days with registered warnings = deemed resignation.
* Work on rest day/holiday → pay rules in 1.4/6.1.
