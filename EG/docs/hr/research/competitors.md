# EG-System HR: Competitor Analysis (Commercial HR / HRMS / Payroll / Attendance)

Prepared 2026-11-04 for the EG (المصرية الألمانية) factory ERP project, HR lifecycle module.
Context: ~100+ workers, mostly blue-collar, plus sales/admin; Gamasa, Dakahlia, Egypt; Arabic RTL first; web dashboard plus mobile app.

## 0. How to read this document (confidence & method)

- Every sourced claim carries a URL. "(unverified)" = vendor marketing only, a single source, a conflicting source, or not found on the official page.
- Prices are exactly as seen on the date above. Currency is stated. Third-party price figures are flagged. Nothing was invented; where a vendor does not publish prices I say so.
- Review evidence (G2/Capterra) is thin for MENA vendors (often <20 reviews), so "weaknesses" for those are mostly inferred from scope gaps, not from review volume.
- Several "best HR software in Egypt" articles are written by vendors who rank themselves first (Bluworks, Zimyo). I used them only for vendor lists and flagged the bias.
- Matrix cells (section 5) are MY classification based on the evidence in sections 2 to 4. "?" means no evidence either way, not "no".
- Egyptian legal items come from law-firm and commentary sites, not the Official Gazette, and they conflict in places (flagged). Have a labour lawyer sign off before encoding any rule.

## 1. Executive summary (the five things management should know)

1. **The Egyptian HR/payroll market is served by a small set of real products**: ZenHR (regional leader, new Cairo office, EGP 150m investment announced) ([Daily News Egypt, 2026-10-20](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/10/20/zenhr-opens-sixth-regional-office-in-cairo-plans-egp-150m-investment-in-egypt/)), Menaitech CURIO/MenaPAY/MenaTA ([Menaitech Egypt FAQ](https://menaitech.com/en-eg/faqs-egypt/)), Bluworks (shift/frontline, founded 2022) ([Bluworks](https://bluworks.io/)), Mozon ([Mozon](https://mozon-tech.com/blog/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1/)), plus Odoo with Egypt payroll, and thin-footprint locals (HITS, LoginHR, Forte HR) ([Zimyo comparison, biased](https://www.zimyo.me/insights/hr-software-in-egypt/)). Gulf leaders (Jisr, Bayzat, Mudad, Qiwa) do **not** cover Egypt.
2. **Nobody verifiably combines all of**: Egyptian payroll law + factory shift/overtime + OHS + contractor/agency labour + piece-rate/incentive + Arabic-first worker UX. ZenHR is strongest on compliance; Bluworks on frontline UX; ZKTeco/Hikvision/Suprema own the hardware but not payroll. This is the opening for an in-house system.
3. **The law just moved.** Labour Law No. 14 of 2025 (in force 1 Sept 2025) changed overtime procedure, probation, notice, leave, disciplinary process and recognised digital records/bank-transfer proof of wages ([Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=842cf812-8c1e-49d4-bfaa-e6401b47b5d2), [Andersen Egypt](https://eg.andersen.com/egypts-labour-law-14-2025/), [Careerians 20 changes](https://www.careerians.com/egyptian-labor-law/egypt-new-labor-law-20-key-changes/)). Vendors are still catching up: ZenHR and Menaitech claim support; one vendor summary says some products still need manual configuration for it (unverified).
4. **Cost anchor**: published numbers are low. Odoo US$8.95 to US$11.20 per user per month (Standard), Zimyo EGP 150 to 300 per user per month with a 25-user minimum (vendor blog, unverified), Bluworks roughly EGP 51 to 82 per employee per month (founder indication via a competitor's blog, unverified), Wanas payroll from EGP 1,500 per month. For 100 workers that is roughly EGP 5k to 30k per month. Build-vs-buy must beat this, so the in-house case rests on fit (factory + Egypt + integration with the rest of EG-System), not on licence savings.
5. **Biggest "we might forget" risks** are legal/operational, not UI: NOSI wage ceilings reset each January (2026: EGP 2,700 to 16,700), overtime post-notification, four-copy contracts, labour-court-only dismissal evidence trail, unbanked workers (payroll cards). See section 7.

- **Other modules**: loans and advances with repayment schedules, end-of-service benefits, custom approval workflows (expenses, overtime, loans, business trips), accounting integrations (QuickBooks, Xero, SAP, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Odoo) ([ZenHR payroll](https://www.zenhr.com/en/modules/payroll)). Third-party "from US$5" is unreliable ([GetApp AU](https://www.getapp.com.au/software/114423/zenhr), unverified).
- **Strengths**: deepest verifiable Egypt statutory coverage; 4.7/161 on Capterra, praised for local-law compliance and responsive support ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/157129/zenHR/reviews/)); SOC 2 Type II claimed ([Zimyo summary](https://www.zimyo.me/insights/hr-software-in-egypt/), unverified).
- **Weaknesses (recurring)**: mobile app missing features, slow performance, glitches, limited integrations, support "can be weak at times" ([G2 summary via search](https://www.g2.com/products/zenhr-solutions-zenhr/reviews), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/157129/zenHR/reviews/)); "missing punch for specific cases" (Capterra reviewer). Described as lighter on learning/advanced performance (Zimyo, biased).
- **Factory/blue-collar/OHS**: no OHS, piece-rate, or contractor-labour feature found. Dopay cards are the nearest blue-collar item.
- **Steal**: approval workflows for every payroll-adjacent request (overtime, loans, encashment); overtime "pay or comp-time" choice; bank templates; payroll cards for unbanked; day-one insurance activation.

### 3.5 Odoo (Enterprise or Community) as deployed in Egypt
- **Pricing (seen 2026-11-04)**: Standard US$11.20 and Custom US$20.90 per user per month; discounted US$8.95 and US$16.40 for the first 12 months on initial users; Light User US$2.90; "all apps for a single fee" (Employees, Payroll, Recruitment, Appraisals, Planning); billing cycle for the quoted numbers not stated on page ([Odoo pricing](https://www.odoo.com/pricing)). Illustrative: 100 users at Standard list = US$1,120 per month (my arithmetic).
- **Egypt payroll**: Egypt appears among payroll countries on Odoo's payroll page, edition not specified ([Odoo Payroll](https://www.odoo.com/app/payroll)); official localisation docs (modules `l10n_eg_hr_payroll` and `_account`) cover 18.75% / 11% social insurance, progressive tax, 21-day leave, tiered sick leave, end-of-service provision fields ([Odoo 19 docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/hr/payroll/payroll_localizations/egypt.html), page truncated in my read). A third-party app at US$79.77 adds Law 148 and income-tax brackets, payslips, journal entries and Form 4 ([Pokutsoft](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_egypt_payroll)). Zimyo says Egyptian statutory payroll is "not fully covered out of the box" (biased: [Zimyo](https://www.zimyo.me/insights/hr-software-in-egypt/)).
- **Attendance/biometrics**: ZKTeco connectors are third-party paid modules (e.g. US$96.95 listing from an Egypt-experienced vendor, ADMS push mappings) ([eK Solutions](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/ek_zk_integration)); no first-party biometric/geofence feature found on [Odoo Employees](https://www.odoo.com/app/employees).
- **Other**: Recruitment, Appraisals, Time Off, Sign (e-signature of contracts), org chart, skills ([Odoo Employees](https://www.odoo.com/app/employees)); third-party HSE modules for incident log, medical checkups, expiring certificates ([Odoo HSE module](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/hse_management)).
- **Weaknesses**: Egypt payroll depends on partner/community quality; Capterra reviewers cite missing configurable payroll features and complex customisation; Odoo ERP 4.2 (1,291 reviews) on Capterra ([Capterra Odoo](https://capterra.com/p/135618/Odoo/reviews/?page=2)). Only technical support on Enterprise per one reviewer.
- **Why it matters**: it is the most likely "buy" alternative for the whole ERP. Your system must beat Odoo on Egypt payroll depth, worker mobile UX in Arabic, and factory attendance.

## 6. Table stakes vs differentiators vs gaps (opinionated, for an Egyptian factory)

### 6.1 Table stakes (must ship in v1 or the system will not replace what ZenHR/Odoo give)
1. **Egyptian payroll engine that is correct to the pound**: Law 148 shares with yearly ceilings/floors (2026: 2,700 to 16,700), progressive income tax, Martyrs'/Emergency Funds, payslips in Arabic, Form 2/4-style reports, gross-up. Editable by rule table (no code deploy) because ceilings change every January.
2. **Attendance from ZKTeco-class devices** (ADMS push/SDK) plus phone GPS fallback; shifts; overtime at +35%/+70% logic; late/early/absence; Ramadan hours; manager approval; lock-and-freeze period before payroll.
3. **Approval workflows** for leave, overtime, loans/advances, missed punch, with audit trail (ZenHR sets the bar here).
4. **Leave engine with Egyptian rules** (first-year 15 days vs 21, sick tiers, unpaid deduction = salary/30).
5. **Core file with documents and expiry alerts** (ID, insurance number, contract, health card).
6. **Bank transfer file export** for your actual bank(s) (get templates from the bank; none published).
7. **Self-service mobile in Arabic**: payslip, balances, requests, schedule.
8. **End-of-service / final settlement calculator**, but parameterised until the law's conflicting summaries are resolved.
9. **Role-based access and audit log** (HR data is sensitive; Egypt's PDPL 151/2020 is cited by Menaitech).

### 6.2 Differentiators (where you can beat the market)
1. **One data model with the factory ERP**: production counts, machine/line assignment, cost-centre labour cost, WPC/PVC line roster. Generic HR tools sit outside the plant; HROne/greytHR show the pattern but are Indian-statutory.
2. **Piece-rate and production-incentive pay** fed from production data, with minimum-wage floor top-up (pattern documented for garment payroll: [MiHCM](https://mihcm.com/blog/payroll-for-the-garment-and-apparel-industry-managing-complexity-at-scale), [Netchex](https://netchex.com/blog/production-line-payroll-piecework-and-incentive-models/)). I found no Egypt HR vendor documenting this (unverified absence).
3. **Insured-wage vs gross-wage reconciliation** (the contract/insured/actual triple check Mudad does in KSA).
4. **Compliance calendar** driven by Law 14/2025 deadlines (see section 7).
5. **Attendance exception queue** with law-aware suggestions (e.g. 10 consecutive absent days triggers a warning workflow before "deemed resignation" threshold).
6. **Worker-grade UX**: icon-first Arabic, large touch targets, offline punch, WhatsApp-style notifications; HROne explicitly targets "first-time smartphone users" ([HROne](https://hrone.cloud/hr-software/manufacturing/)), and ZenHR's mobile gaps are the top review complaint.
7. **Payroll-card / wallet disbursement** for unbanked workers (Dopay partnership shows demand).

### 6.3 Gaps nobody serves well (best opportunities)
1. **OHS inside HR**: accident/near-miss log, PPE issue register, safety-training certificates with expiry, periodic medical fitness, return-to-work. Only Odoo third-party modules and Oracle HRMS show this; no Egyptian HR vendor does (unverified absence).
2. **Contractor / agency / daily-labour management** (head-count by agency, invoicing reconciliation, social-insurance responsibility). Only Indian vendors (HROne, GSHRM) mention contract labour ([HROne](https://hrone.cloud/hr-software/manufacturing/), [GSHRM](https://gshrmpayroll.lovable.app/payroll-for-manufacturing)); none for Egypt.
3. **Discipline workflow matched to Labour Law arts. 137/138/141/148/166**: investigation deadlines, penalty ladder, evidence bundle for labour court. No vendor documents this.
4. **Work-regulations (لائحة تنظيم العمل) generator and acknowledgement tracker** required within 60 days for 10+ workers.
5. **Government filing automation**: NOSI submission and Ministry notifications are manual everywhere I looked; at best "reports". Build export files now, API later.
6. **Bluetooth/QR kiosk plus face attendance with spoof control** at the gate for 100 workers on one device network; vendors sell hardware, not exception handling.
7. **Arabic-first analytics for owners** (labour cost per product line, overtime as % of payroll, absenteeism by shift).

## 8. Ten things competitors do that we might otherwise forget

1. **Yearly NOSI ceiling update with effective-dated rule tables** (ZenHR/Wanas "updates the engine when regulations change") ([Wanas](https://wanas-website.pages.dev/services/payroll)). Effective-date every rate so back-pay recalculation works.
2. **Payroll cards / wallet payment for workers without bank accounts** (ZenHR + Dopay, Bluworks) ([Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/10/20/zenhr-opens-sixth-regional-office-in-cairo-plans-egp-150m-investment-in-egypt/)).
3. **Day-one medical insurance activation and automatic termination on exit** (ZenHR + Amanleek).
4. **Overtime paid-or-comp-time choice with different rates by day type, shift and duration** (ZenHR) plus Egypt's +35%/+70% and 7-day Ministry notification record ([ZenHR payroll](https://www.zenhr.com/en/modules/payroll), [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=842cf812-8c1e-49d4-bfaa-e6401b47b5d2)).
5. **Reduced Ramadan working hours and annual minimum-wage decision tracking** (Menaitech) ([Menaitech](https://menaitech.com/en-eg/faqs-egypt/)).
6. **Four-copy contract output (employer, employee, NOSI, Labour Office) with archive of the signed copy** (Law 14/2025 art. 89; Odoo Sign/Zoho Sign show the e-sign pattern).
7. **Mandatory annual raise of at least 3% of insurance wage as a scheduled review item** and probation-once rule (Law 14/2025 arts. 12 and probation) ([Careerians](https://www.careerians.com/egyptian-labor-law/egypt-new-labor-law-20-key-changes/)).
8. **"Deemed resignation" tracker**: 10 consecutive or 20 separate unjustified absence days (art. 166) feeding the discipline workflow, since dismissal itself needs a labour court (art. 148) ([Careerians](https://www.careerians.com/egyptian-labor-law/egypt-new-labor-law-20-key-changes/)).
9. **Policy acknowledgement and safety-protocol sign-off tracking** (greytHR) and **expiring certificate / medical-check alerts** (Odoo HSE modules, Khula, Oracle HRMS) ([greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/manufacturing), [Odoo HSE](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/hse_management)).
10. **Active-employee billing / light-user concepts and go-live-based pricing** are commercial patterns, but the HR equivalent is a **"light user" tier for workers with a phone only** (Odoo Light User US$2.90; HROne bills active employees only after go-live) ([Odoo pricing](https://www.odoo.com/pricing), [HROne](https://hrone.cloud/hr-software/manufacturing/)). For an in-house system the lesson: make the worker app permission set tiny and fast.

Bonus items: pre-payroll "variance report" vs last month (flag deltas); Zoho/Odoo/QuickBooks-style payroll journal posting to the ERP's accounting; electronic employee file retention 5 years after exit; experience certificate within 15 days of request (art. 175); work-regulations acknowledgement within 60 days of establishing a 10+ worker workplace (art. 137).
